# Reference photo intake - 10 images

This is for private family-and-friends customization. Photos stay outside the public `Sizzle505/Rosie-App` repository.

| # | Requested photograph | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Head-on face, neutral expression, eye-level | Facial proportions and eye spacing |
| 02 | Three-quarter face toward the left | Left ear, snout and muzzle geometry |
| 03 | Three-quarter face toward the right | Symmetry and coat markings |
| 04 | Left profile | Muzzle length and forehead |
| 05 | Right profile | Distinct ear and neck silhouette |
| 06 | Sitting, full body, front | Chest/legs and resting proportions |
| 07 | Standing, full body, side | Back length, tail and leg proportions |
| 08 | Standing, full body, front/three-quarter | Whole-body silhouette |
| 09 | Natural happy or curious expression | Expression range; tongue/mouth if typical |
| 10 | Good daylight image showing coat color and tail | Accurate coat, undercoat, patches |

Aim for original-resolution images in good natural light, avoiding filters, clipped ears/paws, and extreme wide-angle distortion. Phone photos are fine. Do not change the animal's apparent age or breed.

## Intake data
Record privately:
- Preferred app name and the pet's name.
- Breed or mix; approximate age; three distinctive physical traits.
- Personality (one or two sentences), nickname/pronunciation if relevant.
- Preferred URL slug (lowercase, hyphens), optional dedication.
- Consent from the photographer/owner to create and share a personalized website.

**Do not ask for or record home addresses or other unnecessary personal information.**

## Approval gate
First generate a canonical reference from the 10 photos: front, three-quarter, side, seated, standing, and four face expressions. Have the owner approve it. All later art prompts reference the same approved canon. Quality control prioritizes real facial proportions, coat and ear shape, then visual compatibility with existing RosieVerse layouts.

The photo library, canonical master, drafts and approved art remain in private storage. Only the images expressly intended for the publicly accessible pet website should become public.
