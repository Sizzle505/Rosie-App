# Rosie’s Recipes — culinary validation, November 24, 2026

**Status:** editorial benchmarking, not physical test cooking or certification. These are Rosie’s original variants, not reproductions of the linked recipes. Comparable published recipes support techniques and approximate ratios; differences are intentional unless noted below. Time ranges are editorial estimates, not measured results; the upper estimate is stored in totalMinutes. All 25 IDs, titles, descriptions, categories, yields and art remain unchanged. Use standard US measures, a thermometer, and oven doneness checks. Advance thawing and rice preparation are noted where applicable.

Food safety: [FoodSafety.gov safe temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures), [FDA safe food handling](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/safe-food-handling?sf214482469=1). Poultry and reheated leftovers 165°F, ground meat and egg dishes 160°F, fish 145°F; individual eggs firm whites and yolks. Do not taste raw dough or batter. Refrigerate perishables promptly. For baking weights, see [King Arthur recipe success guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/resources/recipe-success-guide) and [brownie texture guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2025/05/21/types-of-brownies).

## Kitchen test priorities
1. Brownies, cookies and pancakes: verify weight-based formulas, spread, yield and baking endpoints with a standard metal pan and calibrated oven.
2. Crispy potatoes and spinach dip: verify texture, browning and salt balance.
3. Risotto, piccata, vodka pasta and peanut noodles: verify sauce consistency and final seasoning.
4. Cook every remaining dish as written and record actual elapsed time, equipment, yield, and final tasting notes before applying any 'kitchen-tested' label.
